Cuida privacy policy

In effect since November 10, 2026 (version 1).

In short

  • Cuida keeps everything on your phone, encrypted. There is no account, no cloud and no ads.
  • Natesoft does not receive, see or sell your data. Cuida does not even have permission to use the Internet.
  • Only what you choose to share leaves the phone, such as a PDF or a backup.

Who makes Cuida

Cuida is an app by Natesoft (Alvaro Nates Huertas), from Colombia. For any question about this policy write to nateshu@yahoo.com.co.

What data you can keep in Cuida

  • Family profiles: name, date of birth, blood type, allergies, conditions, ID number, health insurer, membership number and emergency contacts.
  • Medicines, their schedules and the doses you mark.
  • Readings (blood pressure, sugar, weight and others) and medical appointments.
  • Scanned documents (prescriptions, orders, results) and the text read from them.
  • Claims with your health insurer, with their dates, filing numbers and notes.
  • Your details for claim drafts (name, ID, address, phone and email), if you choose to enter them.

Much of this is health data, which Colombian law calls sensitive data (Law 1581 of 2012). Nobody can force you to provide it: enter only what you want and what is useful to you.

What it is used for

Only to make the app work on your phone: reminding you of doses and appointments, warning you when a medicine is running out or a deadline is near, showing the emergency card, building the report for the doctor and filling in claim drafts.

Where it stays and how it is protected

  • Everything stays on your phone, in an encrypted database. The key is kept by Android's security system (Keystore) and cannot be taken out of the phone.
  • You can require a PIN or your fingerprint to open Cuida.
  • Backups are a file encrypted with the password you choose, and you keep it wherever you want. Without that password nobody can open it, not even Natesoft.
  • Scanned pages stay in the app's private folder, protected by Android's encryption.
  • Android does not copy Cuida's data to the cloud: that option is turned off on purpose.

What leaves the phone

Nothing leaves on its own. Something leaves only when you:

  • share a PDF (the report, a draft or a document) through WhatsApp, email or another app;
  • save a backup in the place you choose;
  • call an emergency contact from the card;
  • set the emergency card as your lock screen wallpaper (anyone who sees your screen will see it).

What you share is subject to the rules of the service you choose.

Google services inside Cuida

  • Document scanner: it runs inside Google Play Services, on your phone. Cuida only receives the images you scan.
  • Text recognition (ML Kit): it works on the phone, offline. This Google library tries to send usage statistics; Cuida removes its Internet permission, so it cannot send them.
  • Google Play: if you ever buy the Pro version or leave a tip, the payment is handled by Google Play under its own policy. Natesoft does not see your payment details.

Permissions Cuida asks for and why

  • Notifications and exact alarms: to remind you at the time of each dose.
  • Start at boot: to schedule reminders again after a restart.
  • Vibrate: so you can feel the reminders.
  • Fingerprint or face: only if you turn on fingerprint lock.
  • Wallpaper: only if you put the emergency card on the lock screen.

Cuida does not ask for location, contacts, microphone or Internet. The scanner's camera is handled by Google Play Services.

Children and teenagers

Cuida is made for adults who look after their own health or their family's. Profiles of children and teenagers are created and managed by a responsible adult. Cuida shows no advertising and does not communicate with anyone.

Your rights

Colombian law gives you the right to know, update, correct and delete your data, and to withdraw your consent. Since everything is on your phone, you do it yourself, right away:

  • to see or correct something, open the profile, medicine, appointment or document and change it;
  • to delete something, use the trash button;
  • to delete everything, uninstall Cuida.

Natesoft has no copy of your data, so it cannot hand it over or delete it for you. If you have questions, write to nateshu@yahoo.com.co. If you believe your rights were violated, you can turn to Colombia's Superintendence of Industry and Commerce.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on Cuida's page, with its date. If Cuida ever needed to send data off your phone, it will ask you first and you will be able to say no.
